# Data Provenance

## Chennai Metropolitan Region Traffic Simulation System

All data in this system is classified using the following schema:

| Classification | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **DOMAIN KNOWLEDGE** | Publicly available, documented, verifiable information used to configure zone characteristics |
| **SYNTHETIC** | Computationally generated data using documented rules and controlled randomness |
| **SYNTHETIC ASSUMPTION** | Synthetic values calibrated from secondary sources (news, official reports) but not directly measured |

---

## 1. Zone Characteristics — DOMAIN KNOWLEDGE

**Source:** Publicly available land-use and transport information about Chennai:
- Chennai Metropolitan Development Authority (CMDA) land-use maps
- Chennai Unified Metropolitan Transport Authority (CUMTA) reports
- Tamil Nadu Urban Infrastructure Financial Services (TNUIFSL) studies
- NHAI/MoRTH road categorisation and corridor descriptions
- Academic literature on Chennai commuting patterns

**Usage:** Defines zone type (office, shopping, intercity_bus etc.), approximate peak hours, festival sensitivity, and weather sensitivity for each of the 17 zones.

**Documented in:** `scripts/chennai_zones.py` (with per-zone rationale comments)

---

## 2. Traffic Volumes — SYNTHETIC

**Source:** Computationally generated by `scripts/build_pipeline.py`

**Method:**
- 24-hour demand curves defined per zone type (office, shopping, IT corridor, etc.)
- Curves are SYNTHETIC ASSUMPTIONS based on documented traffic behaviour patterns
- Base volumes scaled by road capacity (from NHAI/MoRTH guidelines: ~2400 PCU/hr/lane for urban roads)
- Gaussian noise (CV ~6%) applied for realistic variation
- Controlled random seed = 42 for reproducibility

**NOT real-time measured data.** No physical sensors or GPS data are used.

---

## 3. Festival Effects — SYNTHETIC ASSUMPTION

**Source:** Demand multipliers calibrated from secondary sources:
- TNSTC/SETC press releases on extra bus services for festivals
- Retail trade federation reports on Deepavali shopping turnover (The Hindu, 2023)
- Media reports on Koyambedu/Kilambakkam congestion before major festivals

**Method:** Per-zone-type multipliers for day_before / festival_day / day_after.
Values are documented in `scripts/chennai_zones.py` with rationale per festival.

**Documented in:** `docs/festival_mode.md`

---

## 4. Weather Series — SYNTHETIC

**Source:** Monthly probability distributions from India Meteorological Department (IMD) climatological normals for Chennai.

**Method:** Block-generation weather events (1–12 hour duration) sampled from monthly rain probability tables.

**IMD reference:**
- Chennai annual rainfall ~1400mm
- Northeast monsoon (Oct–Dec): ~60% of annual rainfall
- Southwest monsoon (Jun–Sep): ~30%

---

## 5. Weather Effects on Traffic — SYNTHETIC ASSUMPTION

**Source:** Transport research literature:
- Rakha, H. et al. (2008). "Effect of Rainfall on Free-Flow Speed." *Transportation Research Board.*
- FHWA Road Weather Management Program reports (how weather affects traffic operations)

**Conservative estimates applied** — actual Chennai values not available from sensor data.

---

## 6. School Calendar — DOMAIN KNOWLEDGE

**Source:** Tamil Nadu School Education Department general academic calendar pattern.

**Note:** Exact school holiday dates vary by year; approximate pattern used.

---

## 7. Public Transport Activity — SYNTHETIC

**Source:** Generated index (0–1) correlated with peak hour patterns.

**Classification:** SYNTHETIC — no real GPS or transaction data used.
No individual tracking. Aggregated zone-level activity index only.

---

## 8. Parking, Accidents, Construction — SYNTHETIC

**Source:** Rule-based generation:
- Parking: correlated with zone type and time of day
- Accidents: Poisson-like sparse events (~0.3% per road per hour)
- Construction: deterministic sparse pattern per road (hash-based placement)

All are plausible simulation scenarios — not real incident reports.

---

## What This System Does NOT Claim

- It does NOT claim to use real-time Chennai traffic sensor data
- It does NOT use real GPS traces from individual vehicles
- It does NOT use real parking sensor readings
- It does NOT use real accident/incident reports
- All traffic volumes labelled as "predicted" are generated by ML models trained on the synthetic dataset
